Our Services

At PetPrints, we provide a full range of services focused on giving animals in crisis the care and second chances they deserve.

We respond to reports of abandoned, injured, or abused animals. Our team works with the public, local authorities, and other organisations to safely remove animals from dangerous or neglectful situations.

Many of the animals we rescue arrive in poor condition. We provide:

  • Veterinary treatment and surgery
  • Vaccinations and deworming
  • Spay and neuter programmes
  • Nutritional support and recovery care
  • Behavioural rehabilitation where needed

Our ultimate goal is to place every animal into a safe, loving forever home. We carefully screen potential adopters to ensure the best possible match for both the animal and the family.

Interested in adopting?

Foster homes play a vital role in our work. By providing temporary care, foster families free up space at the shelter and give animals a chance to recover in a home environment.

We believe prevention is just as important as rescue. We run educational talks and campaigns focused on:

  • Responsible pet ownership
  • The importance of sterilisation
  • Recognising and reporting animal abuse
  • How the public can support local rescues

Get Involved

There are many ways to support our work. Whether you want to adopt, volunteer, foster, or sponsor an animal, we would love to hear from you.
